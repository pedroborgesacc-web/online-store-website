## 3. Simple explanation

**FREC composition (s. 475.02, verified V-13):** 7 members, appointed by the **Governor** and confirmed by the **Senate**, for **4-year terms**:

- **4 brokers**, each with an active license for the **5** years before appointment;
- **1 broker or sales associate** with an active license for the **2** years before appointment;
- **2 consumer members** who are not and have never been brokers or sales associates;
- at least **one member aged 60 or older**.

The manual adds a quorum of 4 and a limit of two consecutive terms (not verified this session).

**Powers.** *Quasi-legislative*: adopting rules (61J2 F.A.C.). *Quasi-judicial*: deciding discipline in final orders. *Executive/administrative* functions — processing applications, issuing licenses, keeping records, investigating — are carried out by the **DBPR** (Division of Real Estate), which also serves the FREC.

**Neither body can imprison anyone** or award damages; those powers belong to the courts.

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

- 7 members · Governor appoints, Senate confirms · 4-year terms.
- 4 brokers (5 yrs) · 1 broker or SA (2 yrs) · 2 consumers (never licensed) · one member 60+.
- DBPR investigates; FREC disciplines; courts jail.

## 9. Common misconceptions

- "Licensees elect the FREC." The Governor appoints.
- "All members are brokers." Two must never have been licensed.
- "FREC investigates complaints." DBPR does.

## 10. Memory sheet

**4-1-2**: four brokers, one broker-or-associate, two consumers. **5 / 2** years of experience. **60+** member. **4-year** terms.

## 3. Simple explanation

Chapter 475 issues **two license types: sales associate and broker**. A **broker associate** holds a broker license but works under another broker. Only a broker may open an office, hold escrow funds, and be paid directly by the public; a sales associate always acts for an employing broker (or owner-developer).

**Licensure vs registration.** *Licensure* is DBPR's certification that you qualify. *Registration* records your name, address, employer and status. Both must be current for you to operate.

**Group license:** a sales associate works for several **connected** entities of one owner-developer. **Multiple licenses:** a **broker** holds separate licenses for separate, unconnected brokerages. A sales associate can never hold multiple licenses.

**Broker path:** active sales associate license for **24 months during the preceding 5 years** → **72-hour** broker pre-license course → broker exam → **60 hours** of broker post-license education (two 30-hour courses) before the first broker renewal; missing it voids the broker license.

A sales associate may not be an officer or director of a brokerage corporation (that requires a broker license); the manual notes partners/officers who deal with the public must be brokers.

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

- Two license types; broker associate = broker license, works under another broker.
- 24 months in 5 years · 72 hours · 60 hours post-license.
- Group license (connected entities) vs multiple licenses (broker only).

## 9. Common misconceptions

- "Broker associate is a third license type." It is a broker license used under another broker.
- "A sales associate can work for two unrelated brokers." Never.

## 10. Memory sheet

24 in 5, then 72, then 60. Group = connected entities. Multiple = brokers only.

## 3. Simple explanation

**First renewal** comes **18–24 months** after licensure. Before it, a sales associate must complete the **45-hour post-license course**. Missing it makes the license **null and void**; to practice again, the person must requalify from the 63-hour course and exam.

After the first renewal, every **2-year** renewal period requires **14 hours of continuing education** (including core law). Failing to renew makes the license **involuntarily inactive**.

**Reactivation from involuntary inactive (s. 475.183, verified V-11):**

| Time involuntarily inactive | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 12 months or less | 14 hours of commission-prescribed CE |
| More than 12 but less than 24 months | 28-hour commission-prescribed reactivation course |
| More than 24 months | License expires (null and void) |

The study manual mentions only "late CE" — the 28-hour course is a verified addition.

**Status map:**

- **Active** — registered with an employing broker (or, for a broker, an office).
- **Voluntary inactive** — the licensee chose it; may renew indefinitely with 14 hours CE per cycle.
- **Involuntary inactive** — failure to renew; 2-year countdown.
- **Suspended** — disciplinary time-out, up to 10 years.
- **Revoked** — permanent cancellation (narrow exceptions).
- **Null and void** — the license no longer exists.
- **Ineffective** — exists but cannot be used (e.g., associate without an employer).

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

- 45 hours post-license before 1st renewal → miss = void.
- 14 hours CE per 2 years → miss = involuntary inactive.
- Reactivation: ≤12 months 14 hrs · 12–24 months 28 hrs · >24 months void.

## 9. Common misconceptions

- Confusing post-license (void) with CE (inactive) — the most-swapped pair.
- "Involuntary inactive can be fixed any time." Only for 2 years.

## 10. Memory sheet

POST missed → VOID. CE missed → INACTIVE. 1 year → 14; 2 years → 28; past 2 → dead.

## 3. Simple explanation

**Notify the DBPR:**

- **Change of mailing address within 10 days** (also when moving out of state — the license survives the move).
- **Any conviction, guilty plea or nolo contendere plea within 30 days**, whether or not adjudication is withheld.

**One license, one employer.** A sales associate may hold only one license and work for only one broker or owner-employer at a time.

**Changing brokers.** When employment ends, the associate's license cannot be used until the new employer's registration is filed. Showing listings or negotiating in the gap is operating without an effective license. Brokers must notify the DBPR when an associate leaves.

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

- Address change: 10 days. Conviction/plea: 30 days.
- One employer at a time for sales associates.

## 9. Common misconceptions

- "Nolo contendere is not a conviction, so no report." It must be reported.
- "My license is still current, so I can keep working between brokers." It is ineffective until re-registered.

## 10. Memory sheet

Address = 10. Arrest outcome (conviction/plea) = 30. No employer = no work.
