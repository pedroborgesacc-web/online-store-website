## 3. Simple explanation

**Advantages of real estate investment:** income, appreciation, **leverage** (borrowed money magnifying returns on equity), tax shelter. **Disadvantages:** **illiquidity**, management burden, risk.

**Measures:** **cash flow = NOI − debt service**; **cap rate = NOI ÷ price**; equity buildup; appreciation.

**Risks:** business, financial (debt), market, interest-rate, liquidity.

## 10. Memory sheet

Cash flow = NOI − debt service. Cap rate = NOI ÷ price. Leverage cuts both ways.

## 3. Simple explanation

**Business opportunity brokerage** is selling a going concern: real estate (if any) plus **goodwill**, fixtures, equipment and inventory. Under Chapter 475, brokering business opportunities for compensation requires a real estate license, particularly when real property is included (the manual's wording). Business sales are outside the brokerage-relationship disclosure rules except property with four or fewer residential units (V-20).

## 10. Memory sheet

Business = goodwill + assets (+ real estate). Brokering it for pay needs a license.
