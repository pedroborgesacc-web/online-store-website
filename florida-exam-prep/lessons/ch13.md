## 3. Simple explanation

- **FHA** (Federal Housing Administration, part of HUD) **insures** loans made by approved lenders; it never lends. Borrower pays **MIP** (up-front and annual); minimum down payment **3.5%** with qualifying credit.
- **VA** **guarantees** loans to eligible veterans; private lenders lend. The guarantee can replace a down payment (0 down possible); the borrower pays a **funding fee**; value is set by a **Certificate of Reasonable Value (CRV)** (the VA now calls it a Notice of Value; the course uses CRV).
- **Conventional** loans have no government backing; above **80% LTV** the lender requires **PMI**, which can be cancelled as equity grows.

## 10. Memory sheet

FHA insures (MIP) · VA guarantees (funding fee) · conventional >80% LTV → PMI. Government agencies never hand money to buyers.

## 3. Simple explanation

**By repayment:** **amortized** (level payments; interest falls, principal rises); **partially amortized** with a **balloon** final payment; **interest-only** (term) loans; **ARM** — rate = **index (moves) + margin (fixed)**, limited by periodic and lifetime **caps**, sometimes with a teaser rate.

**By purpose:** **package** (real + personal property, e.g., furnished condo); **blanket** (several parcels, with partial **release clauses**); **wraparound** (new junior loan that includes an existing loan; the existing loan must have no due-on-sale clause); **purchase-money** (seller financing); **home equity / HELOC**; **reverse mortgage** (owners **62+**; lender pays the owner; repaid on sale, move-out or death); **construction loan** (draws; replaced by permanent "take-out" financing).

## 10. Memory sheet

ARM = index + margin. Package = real + personal. Blanket = many parcels + release. Reverse = 62+.

## 3. Simple explanation

**Primary market** originates loans: commercial banks, credit unions, savings associations, **mortgage bankers** (lend and often service) and **mortgage brokers** (arrange loans; lend nothing). **Secondary market** buys existing loans so lenders can lend again: **Fannie Mae** and **Freddie Mac** buy loans and issue mortgage-backed securities; **Ginnie Mae** guarantees securities backed by FHA/VA loans. **Intermediation**: pooling deposits for lending. **Disintermediation**: depositors withdraw for better returns elsewhere, starving mortgage lending.

**The Federal Reserve** influences credit with **open-market operations** (buying/selling government securities — the day-to-day tool), the **discount rate**, and **reserve requirements** (bluntest tool). Tight money → higher rates → slower real estate.

**Discount points:** prepaid interest paid to the lender at closing. **1 point = 1% of the loan amount.** Course rule of thumb: each point raises the lender's yield by about **1/8%**.

## 10. Memory sheet

Consumers borrow in the primary market only. Open-market operations = everyday tool. 1 point = 1% of LOAN ≈ +1/8% yield.
