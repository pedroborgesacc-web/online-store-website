## 3. Simple explanation

Real estate markets are **local** and **slow**: the product is immobile and unique, supply responds slowly to demand (construction takes years), and government (zoning, rates, taxes) keeps changing the field. **Demand** drivers: population, income, employment, credit availability. **Supply** drivers: labor, land, materials, construction financing. Rising **vacancy** signals oversupply and softening prices.

**Pricing tools:**

- **CMA** (comparative market analysis) — a licensee's pricing tool using recently sold, currently listed and expired comparables.
- **BPO** (broker price opinion) — ordered by a lender or investor (short sales, portfolio checks).
- **Appraisal** — a licensed or certified appraiser's USPAP-compliant opinion of value.

A licensee may prepare and **charge** for CMAs and BPOs but must **never call them appraisals**.

## 10. Memory sheet

Local, immobile, slow supply. CMA and BPO yes, "appraisal" no.
