## 3. Simple explanation

**The complaint pipeline (manual; partially verified):**

1. **Complaint** filed with the DBPR. It must be **legally sufficient** (alleging facts that would violate a statute or rule). It need not come from a harmed party; anonymous complaints can be investigated if legally sufficient and substantial.
2. **DBPR investigates**; the licensee is generally notified and may respond.
3. **Probable cause panel** (manual: two members, at least one a current FREC member) decides whether probable cause exists.
4. If yes, a **formal (administrative) complaint** is filed.
5. The licensee chooses: **informal hearing** before the FREC (facts not disputed) or **formal hearing** before an **administrative law judge** (facts disputed), who issues a recommended order.
6. The FREC issues the **final order**.
7. The licensee may **appeal within 30 days** to the District Court of Appeal.

Withdrawing the complaint does not stop the process.

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

Order of steps; 30-day appeal.

## 9. Common misconceptions

- Putting probable cause before investigation.
- Thinking the complainant can end the case.

## 10. Memory sheet

Complaint → investigate → probable cause → formal complaint → hearing → final order → appeal (30 days).

## 3. Simple explanation

**Administrative penalties (FREC):** notice of noncompliance (first minor violation); citation (set fine for listed minor violations); administrative fine **up to $5,000 per violation**; probation; **suspension up to 10 years**; **revocation** (permanent, with narrow exceptions); denial of an application or renewal; required education.

**Criminal penalties (courts):** operating without a license = **third-degree felony** (verified V-12), up to 5 years and $5,000 under the general sentencing statutes. Most other s. 475.42 violations are second-degree misdemeanors (partially verified). Rental-list fraud has been a first-degree misdemeanor.

**Violation vocabulary (s. 475.25):** fraud (intentional deception), misrepresentation (false statement of material fact), concealment, false promises, false pretenses, dishonest dealing by trick or scheme, **culpable negligence** (reckless indifference, more than carelessness), breach of trust. These apply in **any business transaction**, not only real estate. Other grounds: commingling, conversion, failure to account and deliver, failure to keep records (**5 years**; at least 2 years after related litigation), operating as a broker while licensed as a sales associate, crimes involving moral turpitude, adjudication of mental incompetence (manual wording: confinement in a mental institution), failure to report a plea or conviction within 30 days.

The FREC never imprisons and never awards damages; fines go to the state.

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

$5,000 per violation · 10 years suspension · unlicensed = 3rd-degree felony · records 5 years.

## 9. Common misconceptions

- "FREC can order restitution like a court." Consumer damages require a civil judgment.

## 10. Memory sheet

Fine 5K · suspend 10 · records 5 · no license = felony.

## 3. Simple explanation

**The Real Estate Recovery Fund (s. 475.484, verified V-17)** pays members of the public who obtain a final civil judgment against a licensee for a violation in a real estate brokerage transaction and cannot collect it.

- Pays actual or compensatory damages only — **not** treble damages, court costs, attorney's fees or interest.
- **$50,000 per transaction** (all claimants combined).
- **$150,000 aggregate** against any one licensee (prorated if exceeded).
- When the Fund pays, the licensee's license is **automatically suspended** until repaid in full plus interest.

**Who cannot recover:** a licensee who acted as a licensee in the transaction (e.g., a commission dispute); the manual also excludes a licensee's spouse in certain claims. A licensee harmed while acting solely as a buyer or seller may recover like any consumer.

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

$50,000 / $150,000 · automatic suspension until repaid with interest.

## 9. Common misconceptions

- "Associates can claim for unpaid commissions."
- "The Fund pays legal fees."

## 10. Memory sheet

50 per deal, 150 per licensee. Public only. Payment = suspension.
