## 3. Simple explanation

Three legal description systems:

- **Metes and bounds** — courses (directions) and distances from a **point of beginning (POB)**, returning to the POB. Used for irregular parcels.
- **Lot and block** — a lot number in a block on a recorded **plat**. The usual description in platted urban subdivisions.
- **Government (rectangular) survey** — townships and sections measured from a principal meridian and base line. Florida's intersect in **Tallahassee**.

A **street address is never a legal description**.

## 10. Memory sheet

Metes & bounds returns to the POB. Lot & block needs a recorded plat. Florida's survey origin is Tallahassee.

## 3. Simple explanation

**Townships** are **6 × 6 miles** = 36 square miles, divided into **36 sections** of **1 square mile = 640 acres** each. Sections are numbered in a serpentine pattern starting in the **northeast** corner (section 1), running west to section 6, then back east, ending with section 36 in the southeast. Section 16 was traditionally reserved for schools.

**Acreage shortcut:** divide 640 by each denominator. NW¼ of the SE¼ = 640 ÷ 4 ÷ 4 = **40 acres**. S½ of the NE¼ = 640 ÷ 2 ÷ 4 = **80 acres**. **AND** joins separate parcels: compute each and add. "N½ of the NW¼ AND the SW¼" = 80 + 160 = **240 acres**. To locate a parcel, read the fractions right to left.

**Conversions:** 1 acre = **43,560 sq ft** · 1 mile = **5,280 ft** · 1 square yard = 9 sq ft · 1 square mile = 640 acres.

**Metric intuition (for understanding only):** 1 acre ≈ 4,047 m² ≈ 0.405 ha; 640 acres ≈ 259 ha.

## 9. Common misconceptions

- Dividing 43,560 by 3 instead of 9 when converting to square yards.
- Multiplying acreages when the description says AND.

## 10. Memory sheet
