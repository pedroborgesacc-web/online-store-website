# Study plan and method

## What still needs the learner's input

The schedule depends on two facts that are not in the materials:

1. **The intended exam date.** The manual's date (18 Aug 2026) has passed.
2. **Realistic study time per day.**

Until they are set (in the app's Settings), the app recommends one activity at a time and suggests a 45–60 minute session.

## A typical session (45–60 minutes)

1. **Warm-up retrieval (5–10 min):** due review questions and flashcards. Answer from memory before seeing anything.
2. **Mistake review (5 min):** open entries in the mistake log, practised through *different* questions on the same objective where possible.
3. **One new lesson (15–20 min):** read it, then close it.
4. **Lesson quiz (10 min):** one question at a time, with confidence ratings.
5. **Calculation practice (5–10 min, on maths days):** worked example → guided → independent → mixed.
6. **Short cumulative quiz (5 min)** once three or more lessons are studied.
7. **Schedule:** the app sets every item's next review automatically.

Take a short break between steps 3 and 4 if attention drops. Do not add more than one or two new legal lessons per day; a lesson only counts as learned once it has been retrieved again on a later day.

## Pacing once the date is known

- Reserve the **last two weeks** (or the last quarter of the time, if shorter) for cumulative practice, weakness training and mock exams.
- Spread the remaining lessons over the other days in prerequisite order (`prerequisite_map.md`).
- If the time is too short for full coverage, prioritize in this order:
  1. High-priority lessons in high-weight chapters: contracts, property rights, mortgages, license law, brokerage relationships, escrow, computations.
  2. Exact-recall facts (Law & numbers).
  3. The learner's weakest objectives.
  4. Medium and low-priority lessons.

  The app will say plainly that coverage is incomplete. A compressed plan does not guarantee mastery.
- **Mock exams:** at most one or two per week in the final phase. The bank has 261 questions, so mocks overlap; review every miss the same day.

## Evidence base, stated honestly

Retrieval practice and spaced (distributed) practice have the strongest research support. Interleaving helps once the individual topics are known, so cumulative and mixed modes unlock after several lessons. Worked examples followed by fading guidance are used for calculations. Confidence ratings support calibration: confident errors get extra attention. None of these methods guarantees a particular result or speed, and the plan adapts to recorded performance.
