## 2. Why it matters

As a sales associate you will advertise all the time: yard signs, portal listings, Instagram reels, WhatsApp statuses, flyers. In Florida each of these is a licensee advertisement, and each one must name your **brokerage**. A missing brokerage name is the most common advertising violation. It is also a frequent exam question, usually disguised as "it was her personal account" or "the name is in her profile".

## 3. Simple explanation

### The purpose

The public must be able to tell that they are dealing with a **real estate licensee**, and which **brokerage** stands behind the ad. The brokerage is the licensed firm responsible for its associates, so its name is the one element that can never be left out.

### The core rules (Rule 61J2-10.025, F.A.C.; V-44)

1. **The brokerage name goes in every ad.** Every real estate advertisement must include the **licensed name of the brokerage firm**. An ad without it is a **blind ad**, whatever the medium: yard sign, newspaper, website, Instagram, WhatsApp status.
2. **No deception.** All advertising must let reasonable people know they are dealing with a real estate licensee. Ads may not be fraudulent, false, deceptive or misleading. The manual adds that an advertised price must be the price agreed with the seller (manual; not yet verified).
3. **Your own name.** If a licensee's personal name appears, **at least the last name** must be used **as registered with the Commission**. A registered "Robert Silva" may advertise as "Bob Silva"; "Bob S." does not use the registered last name.
4. **Internet ads.** The brokerage name must be placed **next to, or directly above or below, the "point of contact information"**. That term means any way to contact the brokerage or the licensee: mailing or street address, email, telephone or fax. Putting the brokerage name on a profile page one click away does not meet the rule.

### Teams and groups (Rule 61J2-10.026, F.A.C., effective 11/1/2021)

A team is a group of licensees who market together under a team name. The team is not a brokerage, and the public must not think it is.

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Same broker | The team works under the same broker and files a designated licensee with the broker |
| Roster | The broker keeps a written roster of team members, updated **at least monthly** |
| Allowed words | The team name may include **"team"** or **"group"** |
| Banned words | **"Brokerage", "Realty", "Real Estate", "Properties", "Company"** or similar words suggesting a separate brokerage |
| Size | In ads, the team name may **not be in larger print** than the registered brokerage's name or logo |
| Clarity | Ads must make clear to reasonable people that they are dealing with a team or group |

### Who is not covered

An **owner who is not a licensee**, advertising her own property, is not placing a licensee ad. She has no brokerage to name.

### Consequences

Leaving out the registered brokerage name can be handled with a **citation** under Rule 61J2-24.002, F.A.C. A citation is a set fine for listed minor violations (L06-2).

### Not (yet) a rule

A 2018–2019 proposed amendment would have required a licensee's name to be no larger than the brokerage name. It would also have barred individual licensees from words like "Brokerage", "Brokers" or "Company". It was **not confirmed as adopted**, so treat it as unverified. The manual's statement that an associate's name "cannot dominate" the brokerage identity falls in the same unverified category. The confirmed size limit applies to **team names**.

Ads are also covered by rules taught elsewhere: fair housing (L07-1), Do Not Call and CAN-SPAM (L07-2), and credit-advertising trigger terms (L07-3).

## 4. Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Advertisement | Any marketing message by a licensee about property or real estate services, in any medium |
| Blind ad | A licensee's ad that does not include the licensed brokerage name |
| Licensed brokerage name | The firm's name as registered with the DBPR/FREC |
| Point of contact information | Any way to contact the brokerage or licensee: address, email, phone, fax |
| Team or group | Licensees under the same broker who market together under a team name |
| Team roster | The broker's written list of team members, updated at least monthly |
| Citation | A set fine for listed minor violations |

## 5. Practical example

**Joana Ferreira** is a sales associate with **Atlantic Palm Realty, LLC** in Miami, and a member of a team. Does each ad comply?

| Ad | Complies? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Yard sign: "FOR SALE — Call Joana, 305-555-0142" | **No** | Blind ad: no brokerage name |
| Instagram reel with her phone number; the brokerage name is only in her bio | **No** | The brokerage name must be next to, above or below the contact details in the ad itself |
| Website: "Jo Ferreira · Atlantic Palm Realty, LLC · 305-555-0142" | **Yes** | Registered last name, and the brokerage name sits beside the contact details |
| Flyer: "Joana F., your Brickell expert · Atlantic Palm Realty, LLC" | **No** | Her registered last name does not appear |
| Team ad: "THE FERREIRA GROUP" in large letters, brokerage name in small print at the bottom | **No** | The team name may not be larger than the brokerage name |
| The team renames itself "Ferreira Realty Team" | **No** | "Realty" suggests a separate brokerage |
| Her neighbour, not a licensee, posts "House for sale by owner, call 786-555-0199" | **Not a licensee ad** | There is no brokerage to name |
| A price $25,000 below the seller's agreed price, "to get calls" | **No** | Misleading. The price must be the one agreed with the seller (manual) |

## 6. Portugal comparison

**Familiar principle.** Mediation advertising is regulated in Portugal too. You may be used to seeing an agency's license identification in its ads. Whether and how Portuguese law requires it *requires verification*.

**Florida rule.** The required element is the **licensed brokerage name**. It must appear in every licensee ad, including an associate's personal social media.

**The difference.** The Florida rule is about the brokerage's **name**. A license number in an ad does not replace it.

**Exam implication.** Answer only with the Florida rule. An associate's personal post still needs the brokerage name. An owner advertising her own property needs none.

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

**Memorize exactly**

- Every real estate ad by a licensee must include the **licensed name of the brokerage firm**. Without it, the ad is a **blind ad** (Rule 61J2-10.025(1)).
- Ads may not be fraudulent, false, deceptive or misleading (Rule 61J2-10.025(1)).
- Licensee's personal name: at least the **last name as registered** with the Commission (Rule 61J2-10.025(2)).
- Internet ads: the brokerage name goes **next to, or directly above or below, the point of contact information** (Rule 61J2-10.025(3)(a)).
- Team ads (Rule 61J2-10.026, effective 11/1/2021):
  - same broker, with a designated licensee filed with the broker;
  - roster updated **at least monthly**;
  - "team" or "group" allowed;
  - "Brokerage", "Realty", "Real Estate", "Properties", "Company" or similar words banned;
  - team name **not larger** than the brokerage's name or logo;
  - clear to reasonable people that it is a team.
- A missing brokerage name can be cited under Rule 61J2-24.002.
- Advertised price = the price agreed with the seller (manual; not yet verified).

**Understand**

- The team rules are in **Rule 61J2-10.026**, not in 10.025 as the manual says.
- The 2018–2019 proposed size and wording limits for individual licensees are unverified, and so is the manual's "cannot dominate". The size limit for team names is confirmed.
- The rule is about licensee advertising. An owner who is not a licensee needs no brokerage name.

## 9. Common misconceptions

- **"My personal account is exempt."** It is not. A licensee's ad on any account or medium needs the brokerage name.
- **"The brokerage name in my bio or profile is enough."** In an internet ad it must sit next to, above or below the contact information.
- **"My license number replaces the brokerage name."** The rule requires the brokerage **name**.
- **"Team names are not allowed."** They are allowed. They may use "team" or "group" but not "Realty", "Real Estate", "Properties", "Company" or "Brokerage".
- **"A nickname is illegal."** A nickname is allowed if the registered **last name** appears.
- **"An owner selling by herself must name a brokerage."** An owner who is not a licensee has none to name.
- **"An individual licensee's name may never be bigger than the brokerage name."** That was proposed but is unverified. The confirmed size rule applies to team names.

## 10. Memory sheet

- **No firm name = blind ad**, in any medium, including personal social media.
- Personal name: at least the **registered last name**.
- Internet: firm name **next to, above or below** the contact info.
- Teams (61J2-10.026): "team" or "group" only. No Realty, Real Estate, Properties, Company or Brokerage. Never larger than the brokerage name. Roster updated **monthly**.
- Owners who are not licensees are not placing licensee ads.
- Missing brokerage name → **citation** possible (61J2-24.002).
