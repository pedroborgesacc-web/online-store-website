## 2. Why it matters

These laws reach three parts of your working day:

- **your office**, which must be accessible to the public (ADA);
- **your buyers' loans**, which must be granted without discrimination (ECOA) and are reported to regulators (HMDA);
- **your prospecting** by phone, email and fax (Do Not Call, CAN-SPAM, junk fax rules).

Prospecting by phone and email is routine in Florida brokerage, so these rules affect most licensees every week.

On the exam (Section VII, **3%**, shared with L07-1, L07-3 and L07-4), the questions are of two kinds:

- **matching**, such as "a wheelchair ramp at the office: which law?";
- **numbers**: **18 months / 3 months** for Do Not Call and **10 business days** for email opt-outs.

## 3. Simple explanation

### Four laws, four subjects

The exam describes a situation and asks which law governs. Find the **subject** first.

| Subject in the question | Law |
|---|---|
| **Housing**: selling or renting a dwelling | Fair Housing Act (L07-1) |
| **Credit**: a loan application | **ECOA**, the Equal Credit Opportunity Act |
| **Public places and businesses**: an office, a store | **ADA**, the Americans with Disabilities Act |
| **Lending data**: reports on where and to whom lenders lend | **HMDA**, the Home Mortgage Disclosure Act |

**ADA.** Places of **public accommodation** and commercial facilities, including **brokerage offices**, must be accessible to people with disabilities. Barriers must be removed where this is **readily achievable**. The ADA governs business premises, **not private homes**. Housing is fair housing territory.

**ECOA.** A creditor (lender) may not discriminate against a credit applicant on any of these bases:

1. **race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, or age** (if the applicant is old enough to sign a binding contract);
2. **receipt of public assistance income**;
3. the applicant's **good-faith exercise of consumer credit rights**.

These bases do not stop a lender from judging whether the applicant can repay. Insufficient income, a poor history of paying debts on time and lack of funds for the down payment are not protected bases, so they can be legitimate reasons to deny a loan.

**HMDA.** Lenders **report data** about their mortgage lending. The law is about reporting, not about banning anything. The data reveals patterns such as **redlining** (L07-1).

**CFPB.** The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau administers the TRID disclosure rules (L07-3).

### Marketing rules

**Do Not Call.** A telemarketing call is a call made to sell something; for you, that means prospecting for listings or buyers. You may not make such a call to a number on the **National Do Not Call Registry**, or on Florida's list, unless one of two exceptions applies:

- **written permission** from the consumer; or
- an **established business relationship** (EBR) with your firm.

Two federal rules define the EBR, with almost the same windows:

| Rule | After a purchase or transaction | After an inquiry or application |
|---|---|---|
| FCC rule (47 CFR 64.1200) | **18 months** | **3 months** |
| FTC Telemarketing Sales Rule (16 CFR 310.2(q)), since May 16, 2024 | **540 days** | **90 days** |

Under the FCC rule, the relationship must come from a voluntary two-way communication and must not have been ended by either party. A consumer's request to **your company** not to call ends it for telemarketing, even inside the window.

**CAN-SPAM** (commercial email):

- Honor an opt-out (unsubscribe) within **10 business days**. Once someone opts out, you also may not sell or transfer their address.
- The opt-out link or reply address must keep working for **at least 30 days** after the email is sent.
- Opting out cannot cost a fee or require more than an email address, a reply email, or a visit to a single web page.
- Every commercial email must include a **valid physical postal address**: a current street address, a registered USPS post office box, or a private mailbox registered with a commercial mail receiving agency.
- Header and subject lines must be truthful, and the email must be identified as an advertisement where required.

**Junk Fax Prevention Act.** Do not send unsolicited fax advertisements without an established business relationship.

## 4. Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ADA | Americans with Disabilities Act: accessibility of public accommodations and commercial facilities |
| Public accommodation | A place of business open to the public, such as a brokerage office |
| Readily achievable | The ADA test for when barriers at business premises must be removed |
| ECOA | Equal Credit Opportunity Act: no discrimination in credit |
| Public assistance income | Income from a government assistance program; a protected basis under ECOA |
| HMDA | Home Mortgage Disclosure Act: lenders report lending data |
| CFPB | Consumer Financial Protection Bureau; administers TRID |
| Telemarketing | Calling to sell goods or services, including prospecting calls by licensees |
| Established business relationship (EBR) | A recent transaction or inquiry that allows calls to a registered number for a limited time |
| CAN-SPAM | Federal law on commercial email: opt-outs, postal address, truthful headers |
| Opt-out | A recipient's request to stop receiving commercial email |

## 5. Practical example

**Rafael**, a sales associate in Miami, plans a morning of calls. Every number below is on the National Do Not Call Registry.

| Contact | Call? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Couple who closed a purchase through Rafael's brokerage **14 months** ago | **Yes** | Inside 18 months (and inside 540 days) |
| Woman who emailed about one of his listings **2 months** ago | **Yes** | Inside 3 months (and inside 90 days) |
| Man who asked about a rental **5 months** ago | **No** | The inquiry window has closed |
| Owner of an **expired listing with another brokerage** | **No** | No relationship with Rafael's firm |
| Past client from **10 months** ago who told the office "please stop calling me" | **No** | A company-specific request ends the relationship |
| FSBO seller who signed Rafael's written permission form at a homeowners' fair | **Yes** | Written permission |

That afternoon, a newsletter subscriber clicks "unsubscribe". Rafael's brokerage must stop commercial emails to her within **10 business days** (business days, not calendar days) and may not sell her address.

His office in Coral Gables needs an accessible entrance: that is the **ADA**. His buyer's lender may not refuse her because part of her income is public assistance: that is **ECOA**.

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

**Memorize exactly**

- Housing = **Fair Housing Act** · credit = **ECOA** · public accommodations = **ADA** · lending data = **HMDA**.
- ECOA bases: race, color, religion, national origin, sex, **marital status**, **age** (if able to contract), **public assistance income**, **good-faith exercise of consumer credit rights** (V-61).
- Do Not Call EBR: **18 months** after a purchase or transaction; **3 months** after an inquiry (FCC rule). The FTC rule since **May 16, 2024**: **540 days / 90 days** (V-58). **Exam answer: 18 months / 3 months, unless the question uses days.**
- A company-specific do-not-call request ends the relationship (V-58).
- CAN-SPAM: opt-out honored within **10 business days**; valid **physical postal address** in every commercial email; opt-out working for **at least 30 days** after sending (V-59).

**Understand**

- Correction: older books give only "18 months / 3 months". That is still literally correct under the FCC rule, but the FTC rule's wording changed in 2024 to 540 days / 90 days. These are close, but not identical, to 18 and 3 months.
- From the manual only (manual; not yet verified): the ADA rules (public accommodation, readily achievable barrier removal, not private homes); HMDA's description; the CFPB administering TRID; the Florida do-not-call list; the written-permission exception; truthful headers and ad identification under CAN-SPAM; and the junk fax rule.

## 9. Common misconceptions

- **"A wheelchair ramp at the office is a fair housing issue."** It is an **ADA** issue. Fair housing covers dwellings.
- **"The ADA requires sellers to make private homes accessible."** The ADA governs public accommodations and commercial facilities, not private homes.
- **"Another brokerage's expired listing is fair game."** You have no relationship with that owner. Do not call a registered number.
- **"An inquiry gives you 18 months."** An inquiry gives **3 months**; 18 months follows a transaction.
- **"Past customers on the registry can be called forever."** Only within 18 months of the transaction, and never after they ask your company to stop.
- **"540 days is a wrong answer."** It is correct under the FTC rule since 2024. Choose by the units the question uses.
- **"ECOA stops a lender from turning down anyone with low income."** Insufficient income, poor payment history and lack of down-payment funds are not protected bases. Receiving public assistance income is.
- **"ECOA and the Fair Housing Act protect the same classes."** ECOA adds marital status, age, public assistance income and the exercise of credit rights. Familial status and handicap are fair housing classes.
- **"HMDA prohibits redlining."** HMDA is a **reporting** law; its data exposes redlining.
- **"CAN-SPAM gives 30 days to honor an opt-out."** **10 business days.** Thirty days is how long the opt-out mechanism must keep working.

## 10. Memory sheet

- **Housing = FHA · Credit = ECOA · Access = ADA · Data = HMDA.**
- ECOA adds: **marital status, age, public assistance, exercising credit rights**.
- Do Not Call: **18 after a deal, 3 after a question** (FTC: 540 / 90 days). "Stop calling me" ends it. Another firm's expired listing = no call.
- CAN-SPAM: opt-out in **10 business days** · postal address · opt-out works **30 days**.
- Fax ads: only with an established relationship.
