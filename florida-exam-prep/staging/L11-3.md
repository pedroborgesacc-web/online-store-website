## 2. Why it matters

The listing agreement gives your brokerage the right to market a property and, above all, the right to be paid. Its type decides whether a commission is owed when the seller finds the buyer alone. A missing expiration date, a self-renewing clause or a late copy for the seller can lead to discipline by the Florida Real Estate Commission (FREC), whatever the seller agreed to.

On the exam (Section 11, 12% of the questions, V-02), listing questions are predictable:

- The seller finds her own buyer: is a commission owed? The answer depends on one word, **agency** or **right**.
- Which clause is NOT allowed in a written listing? (Automatic renewal.)
- How soon must the seller receive a copy? (24 hours.)
- Which event does NOT end a listing? (The listing associate moving to another firm.)

## 3. Simple explanation

**Source status.** The written-listing requirements are verified (s. 475.25(1)(r), F.S.; V-49). The listing types, commission rules and termination rules come from the study manual *(manual; not yet verified)*.

### Step 1: A listing is an employment contract with the brokerage

A **listing agreement** is an employment (personal-service) contract: the seller (the **principal**) hires the **brokerage** to find a buyer *(manual; not yet verified)*. Three consequences follow:

- The listing belongs to the **brokerage**, not to the individual sales associate. If the associate moves to another firm, the listing stays with the brokerage.
- Because it is a personal-service contract, it is **not assignable** to another brokerage without the seller's consent.
- The money path is fixed: seller → brokerage → associate. A sales associate may be paid only by the employing broker (PT-06; manual; not yet verified).

### Step 2: The four listing types and when the commission is earned

| Type | Brokerages | If the seller finds a buyer alone | If anyone else produces the buyer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Open listing** | Any number | **No commission** | Only the brokerage that is the **procuring cause** is paid; the others get nothing |
| **Exclusive agency** | One | **No commission**: the seller keeps the right to sell personally | The listing brokerage earns it |
| **Exclusive right of sale** | One | **Commission owed** to the listing brokerage | The listing brokerage earns it: it is paid **no matter who sells** |
| **Net listing** | — | — | The broker keeps whatever the sale brings **above the seller's set net price** |

All rows: manual; not yet verified.

**Procuring cause** means the broker whose efforts started an uninterrupted chain of events that led to the sale. It decides open listings, where several brokers compete and only one is paid. The manual calls exclusive right of sale the strongest and standard form: it protects the brokerage even against the seller's own efforts.

**Net listings.** The manual says a net listing is **legal in Florida but conflict-laden** *(manual; not yet verified)*. Why the conflict? If the broker advises the seller on the net price, the broker earns more the lower that net is set, so the broker's interest pulls against the client's.

### Step 3: Florida's rules for written listings (verified, V-49)

Under **s. 475.25(1)(r), F.S.** (F.S. = Florida Statutes), FREC may discipline a licensee who fails to include the required terms in a written listing agreement or to deliver a copy on time. Every **written** listing agreement must contain:

1. a **definite expiration date**;
2. a **description of the property**;
3. the **price and terms**;
4. the **fee or commission**;
5. the **signature of the principal(s)**.

It **may not contain** any provision requiring the person signing to **notify the broker of an intention to cancel after the definite expiration date**. The statute never uses the words "automatic renewal", but in effect it bars self-renewing listings that need a cancellation notice; exam materials call this **"no automatic renewal"**. A **copy** must be given to the **person signing within 24 hours** of obtaining the written listing agreement.

**Why these rules?** A definite end date and the ban on cancel-by-notice clauses stop sellers from being locked in by fine print. The 24-hour copy lets the seller know exactly what was signed.

**Must every listing be written?** These requirements apply to **written** listings; the statute does not require every listing to be in writing (V-49). The manual states that a listing need not be written to be valid unless its term exceeds one year, so an oral open listing can be enforceable *(manual; not yet verified; compare the statute of frauds, which covers contracts that cannot be performed within one year, also not yet verified, V-26)*. In practice, put every listing in writing.

### Step 4: How listings end

A listing terminates by *(manual; not yet verified)*:

| Way | Example |
|---|---|
| **Performance** | The brokerage finds the buyer and the sale is completed |
| **Expiration** | The definite expiration date passes |
| **Mutual agreement** | Seller and brokerage agree to cancel |
| **Abandonment** | One side abandons the contract |
| **Destruction of the property** | The house burns down |
| **Death or incapacity of either party** | The seller dies or becomes incapacitated |

What does **not** end it: the listing associate leaving the brokerage, because the contract is with the brokerage. Nor can it be passed to another brokerage without the seller's consent.

## 4. Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Listing agreement | Employment (personal-service) contract between seller and brokerage |
| Open listing | Listing given to several brokers; only the procuring broker is paid; owner may sell free |
| Exclusive agency listing | One brokerage; owner keeps the right to sell without commission |
| Exclusive right of sale | One brokerage earns the commission no matter who sells |
| Net listing | Broker keeps any amount above the seller's net price |
| Procuring cause | The broker whose efforts started an uninterrupted chain of events leading to the sale |
| Automatic renewal | Exam shorthand for a clause requiring notice to cancel after expiration; not allowed |

## 5. Practical example

**A listing in Fort Lauderdale.** On Tuesday at 10:00 Maria signs an exclusive-right-of-sale listing with Coastal Realty. Her sales associate is João.

| Situation | Answer |
|---|---|
| The draft says: "This agreement renews for successive 6-month terms unless the seller gives 30 days' written notice." | Not allowed. It requires notice to cancel after the expiration date (s. 475.25(1)(r), V-49). Replace it with a definite expiration date. |
| When must Maria receive her copy? | Within 24 hours of obtaining the signed listing: by **Wednesday at 10:00**. Hours, not business days (V-49). |
| Maria's cousin buys the house directly from her, with no help from Coastal. | **Commission owed** to Coastal: exclusive right of sale pays no matter who sells *(manual; not yet verified)*. |
| Same facts, but the listing was exclusive agency. | **No commission**: Maria kept the right to sell personally *(manual; not yet verified)*. |
| Instead, Maria gave open listings to three brokerages, and Broker B's showing started the chain of events that led to the sale. | Only Broker B, the procuring cause, is paid. Had Maria sold to her cousin herself, no brokerage would be paid *(manual; not yet verified)*. |
| João moves to another brokerage in month two. | The listing **stays with Coastal Realty** and does not end. It does not follow João: the contract is between Maria and Coastal, and a listing is not assignable to another brokerage without consent *(manual; not yet verified)*. |
| At closing, grateful Maria hands João a $500 gift card. | A violation unless it goes through his broker; an associate is paid only by the employing broker (PT-06; manual; not yet verified). |

If Coastal is to act as Maria's single agent, the single agent notice must be given before or at the time of the listing agreement, or before showing the property, whichever comes first (s. 475.278, V-48; see L04-3).

## 6. Portugal comparison

**The familiar principle.** In Portugal the **contrato de mediação imobiliária** must be in writing. Missing mandatory content makes it null, and the mediation company cannot invoke that nullity. Contract models with general clauses need IMPIC approval or must follow the official model (Portaria n.º 228/2018) (Lei 15/2013, art. 16; PT-05, verified). Remuneration is due on the conclusion and perfection of the deal the mediation aimed at; under exclusivity it remains due if the deal fails for reasons attributable to the client (art. 19), and courts require a causal link between the mediator's work and the deal (PT-06, verified).

**The Florida rule.** A listing is an employment contract between the seller and the **brokerage**. Written listings need a definite expiration date, the property description, price and terms, the fee and the principal's signature; no cancel-by-notice ("automatic renewal") clause; and a copy to the signer within 24 hours (V-49). The listing type decides when the commission is earned *(manual; not yet verified)*. Payment flows seller → brokerage → associate (PT-06).

**The precise difference.**

- Portugal requires the written form. Florida's statute regulates **written** listings but does not require every listing to be written (V-49).
- Florida's verified consequence for a missing term or a late copy is **discipline of the licensee** (V-49), not the Portuguese nullity rule.
- The Portuguese causal-link idea resembles Florida's **procuring cause** in open listings. But under a Florida exclusive right of sale the brokerage is paid even when the seller sells with no help at all. Whether a Portuguese exclusive contract pays when the owner sells alone requires verification.
- In Florida, a sales associate can never accept payment directly from a customer (PT-06; manual; not yet verified).

**Exam implication.** When the owner finds the buyer: exclusive right of sale → commission; exclusive agency → none; open → only the procuring broker. Memory aid: **RIGHT of sale = paid regardless; AGENCY = owner keeps the right to sell free.**

## 7. Legal and numerical requirements

**Memorize exactly**

- Written listing (s. 475.25(1)(r), V-49): definite expiration date · property description · price and terms · fee or commission · principal's signature · no clause requiring notice to cancel after expiration (no automatic renewal) · copy to the person signing within **24 hours**.
- Open → procuring cause only; owner sells free. Exclusive agency → owner sells free. Exclusive right of sale → paid no matter who sells *(manual; not yet verified)*.
- Net listing: broker keeps the excess over the seller's net; legal in Florida per the manual, but conflict-laden *(manual; not yet verified)*.
- Termination: performance, expiration, mutual agreement, abandonment, destruction of the property, death or incapacity of either party *(manual; not yet verified)*.

**Understand**

- The listing is with the brokerage: an associate's departure does not end it, and it is not assignable to another brokerage without consent *(manual; not yet verified)*.
- The statute regulates written listings but does not require writing; its sanction is license discipline (V-49). The manual's view: writing is needed only for a term over one year *(manual; not yet verified, V-26)*.
- Commission rates are negotiable; a question describing a "standard rate" among brokers suggests price fixing (PT-06; manual; not yet verified).

## 9. Common misconceptions

- **"The listing is my contract, so it moves with me."** No. It is the brokerage's contract with the seller.
- **"Exclusive agency and exclusive right of sale are the same."** No. Under exclusive agency the owner can sell personally without paying a commission.
- **"An automatic renewal is fine if the seller initials it."** No. A written listing may contain no clause requiring notice to cancel after the expiration date (V-49).
- **"The seller's copy is due within 3 business days."** No. Within 24 hours (V-49).
- **"Every listing must be in writing by statute."** The listing statute, s. 475.25(1)(r), regulates written listings; it does not require writing (V-49). The manual says writing is needed only for terms over one year *(manual; not yet verified)*.
- **"Net listings are illegal in Florida."** The manual says they are legal, though conflict-laden *(manual; not yet verified)*.
- **"A seller may tip the associate directly."** No. Money flows through the broker (PT-06).

## 10. Memory sheet

- Listing = personal-service contract with the **brokerage**. Not assignable without consent; associate leaving ≠ termination.
- **RIGHT of sale = always paid. AGENCY = seller can sell free. OPEN = only the procuring cause.** Net = excess over the seller's net (legal per manual; conflict) *(manual; not yet verified)*.
- Written listing (V-49): end date · description · price and terms · fee · principal's signature · **no auto-renew** · **copy within 24 hours**.
- Ends by: performance · expiration · mutual agreement · abandonment · destruction · death or incapacity *(manual; not yet verified)*.
